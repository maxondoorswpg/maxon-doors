Custom Garage Doors Winnipeg

Have a non-standard rough opening or architectural custom vision? Maxon Doors stocks North America's biggest inventory of standard garage doors and manufactures custom sizes right here in Winnipeg with heavy-duty 2-inch panel thickness and R-17.4 continuous insulation.

Winnipeg Custom Fabrication

Built to Your Exact Rough Opening

While standard sizes such as 9x7 or 16x7 account for many residential homes, custom architectural projects, heritage garage retrofits, and acreage workshops often demand unique dimensions. Forcing a standard door into an off-size opening leads to unsightly exterior trim filler boards, poor perimeter seals, and air leaks.

At 50 Mandalay Drive in Winnipeg, Maxon Doors customizes door dimensions with precision 2-inch panel construction, R-17.4 continuous polyurethane insulation, matching interior/exterior finishes, and American standard hardware tailored precisely to your building plans.

Custom Spring Counterbalance Calibration Every custom door is paired with a mathematically calculated double torsion spring system matched to the exact weight and lift path of the door, ensuring long cycle life and effortless motor lift.

Applications & Capabilities

When Do You Need a Custom Garage Door?

Non-Standard Heights (8'3", 9', 10')

Accommodate lifted pickup trucks, commercial vans, camper trailers, or boats without having to modify your structural header.

Non-Standard Widths (10', 12', 14', 15')

Fit single and 1.5-car custom garage framing found in older Winnipeg character neighborhoods like River Heights, Wolseley, and West Kildonan.

Oversized Double Doors (18x8, 20x8)

Expansive double car entries for luxury executive residences and rural properties throughout Headingley and East St. Paul.

Architectural Full-View Glass Grids

Custom glass panel sizing and mullion spacing to harmonize perfectly with the window lines and fenestration of modern homes.

High-Lift & Vertical Lift Track

Maximize interior garage ceiling clearance for vehicle hoists, indoor golf simulators, or overhead mezzanine storage.

Same Colour on Both Sides

Custom flush panels built with identical high-grade baked enamel finishes inside and out for a fully finished heated garage.
