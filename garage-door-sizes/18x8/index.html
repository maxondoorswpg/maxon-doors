Who is an 18x8 Garage Door For?

An 18' x 8' garage door measures 216 inches wide by 96 inches high. While standard 16-foot double doors can feel snug when maneuvering two wide modern vehicles (such as heavy-duty pickups, wide SUVs, or vehicles with broad side mirrors), an 18-foot width provides an expansive entrance that completely eliminates parking stress.

Engineering an 18-foot door demands exceptional structural integrity. Maxon Doors reinforces 18x8 doors with heavy-gauge horizontal reinforcement struts, industrial end stiles, and custom-calibrated commercial double torsion springs to ensure smooth travel without deflection.
