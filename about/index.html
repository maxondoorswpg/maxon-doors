Who We Are

Maxon Doors was founded to fulfill a clear mission in the Manitoba construction market: deliver high-performance, architecturally refined garage doors engineered for severe climate resilience, without inflated dealer markups and logistical delays.

As a local Winnipeg company, we understand that a garage door is not merely a decorative element—it is an integral component of your home's thermal barrier, security, and day-to-day convenience.

What We Supply & Custom Build

Maxon Doors specializes in modern, high-performance garage door designs:

  • Modern Flush Garage Doors: Smooth, seamless flat-panel profiles engineered for contemporary prairie homes, available in Midnight Black and Polar White, finished in matching colors on both the interior and exterior.
  • All-Glass / Full-View Garage Doors: Commercial-grade extruded aluminum frame doors fitted with tempered frosted, tinted, or clear safety glass.
  • 2" Thick R-17.4 Insulated Doors: Continuous polyurethane thermal barrier doors that prevent cold air drafts during -40°C winter conditions.
  • Custom Architectural Doors: Tailored dimensional panels built for non-standard opening widths, high ceilings, or unique building requirements in Winnipeg.

Quality & Construction Standards

Every garage door supplied or custom-crafted by Maxon Doors in Winnipeg is assembled to American standard engineering tolerances:

  • 2-Inch Panel Thickness: Heavy-duty commercial-grade panel depth providing exceptional rigidity against high prairie winds.
  • R-17.4 Continuous Polyurethane: High-density injected foam core creating an unbroken thermal boundary against -40°C Manitoba cold snaps.
  • Double Torsion Springs: Balanced dual spring systems calculated to door panel mass for smooth, high-cycle performance.
  • Same Colour Both Sides: High-grade baked finishes on interior and exterior skins for a completed luxury interior aesthetic.
  • Heavy Gauge Hardware: Precision steel hinges, sealed ball-bearing nylon rollers, and reinforced vertical/horizontal tracks.
  • Perimeter Weather Sealing: Low-temperature flexible seals that maintain an airtight bond to floors even at -40°C.
