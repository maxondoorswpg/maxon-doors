Who Flush Garage Doors Are For

Flush garage doors are engineered for homeowners, architects, and custom home builders seeking an understated, contemporary appearance. Unlike traditional raised colonial panels, our flush doors feature a smooth, unbroken plane that accentuates horizontal lines, concrete textures, dark stone, and natural cedar siding.

If you have an attached heated garage or a living space situated above the garage, the Maxon Flush Door provides high thermal insulation, structural rigidity, and quiet operation in cold weather with its heavy-duty 2-inch panel thickness.

Direct Supplier & Custom Sizing Advantage Maxon Doors maintains North America's biggest garage door inventory right here in Winnipeg, plus custom size manufacturing capability at 50 Mandalay Drive. You get standard sizes immediately and custom openings built without out-of-province delays.

Standard Engineering Specifications

Features of Maxon Flush Garage Doors

Every flush garage door supplied by Maxon Doors includes these premium standards:

2-Inch Panel Thickness & R-17.4 Insulation

Heavy-duty 2-inch (50.8 mm) commercial-grade thickness with continuous high-density polyurethane core, providing superior structural rigidity and insulation against Manitoba winters.

Same Colour on Both Sides

Enjoy identical high-grade baked enamel finish on both the exterior facade and interior garage face. No primer-white inside skins.

Double Torsion Spring System

Equipped with commercial-grade dual torsion springs on a steel torsion shaft, providing balanced lift cycles and long-term spring life in Manitoba winters.

American Standard Hardware

Heavy gauge steel hinges, precision ball-bearing rollers, and heavy-duty track hardware for whisper-quiet and reliable door movement.

All Standard & Custom Sizes

Available in stock sizes (9x7, 9x8, 16x7, 16x8, 18x8) and custom dimensional manufacturing in our Winnipeg facility.

Sub-Zero Thermal Seals

Flexible bottom astragal seal and perimeter weatherstripping remain pliable in -40°C Winnipeg cold snaps to keep out snow and wind.
