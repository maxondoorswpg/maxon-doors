Sizing Strategy

Choosing Between 7-Foot and 8-Foot Heights

In modern Canadian homes, door height has become a vital consideration due to the size of contemporary vehicles:

7-Foot High Doors (7'0" / 84 inches)

The standard North American residential garage door height for past decades.

  • Best For: Sedans, hatchbacks, compact crossovers, and garages with lower ceiling clearances.
  • Clearance Consideration: May be tight for lifted pickup trucks, rooftop ski cargo boxes, or roof-mounted bike racks.
  • Sizes: 9' x 7' Single • 16' x 7' Double

8-Foot High Doors (8'0" / 96 inches)

The modern builder standard for Manitoba homes, accommodating modern trucks and SUVs.

  • Best For: Full-size pickups (F-150, Silverado, Ram), large SUVs (Tahoe, Expedition), rooftop carriers.
  • Clearance Consideration: Provides an additional 12 inches of vertical headroom, preventing vehicle roof antenna collisions.
  • Sizes: 9' x 8' Single • 16' x 8' Double • 18' x 8' Wide
