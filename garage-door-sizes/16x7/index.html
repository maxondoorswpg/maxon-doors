Who is a 16x7 Garage Door For?

A 16' x 7' garage door (192 inches wide by 84 inches high) is the most widespread double-car garage door specification in Canada. It allows two standard passenger vehicles, sedans, crossovers, or smaller SUVs to park comfortably side-by-side through a single continuous opening.

Because a 16-foot wide door spans a significant distance, structural rigidity is paramount. Cheap non-insulated or single-skin doors frequently sag in the center under their own weight. Maxon Doors' composite polyurethane steel core bonds into an exceptionally rigid panel, reinforced with heavy-duty horizontal struts and a commercial double torsion spring system.
