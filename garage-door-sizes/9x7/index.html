Dimension Overview

Who is a 9x7 Garage Door For?

A 9' x 7' garage door (108 inches wide by 84 inches high) is the classic single-car opening width found across Winnipeg neighborhoods. It provides plenty of mirror-to-mirror clearance for passenger sedans, hatchbacks, compact SUVs, and sports cars.

Homes with a tandem single garage or twin single garage doors (two 9x7 doors side by side with a center support pillar) rely on 9x7 doors. Maxon Doors keeps 9x7 doors in stock in Winnipeg for rapid replacement or contractor pickup.
