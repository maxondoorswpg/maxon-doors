Engineered Specifically for Winnipeg & Manitoba Winters

Winnipeg experiences some of the most demanding climatic extremes in North America. Winter temperatures routinely reach -35°C to -40°C with severe wind chill and frost heave. Standard, uninsulated, or lightweight builder-grade doors rapidly lose interior heat, freeze to thresholds, and suffer spring fatigue.

R-17.4 Thermal Efficiency

Continuous polyurethane injection creates an unbroken thermal envelope between steel skins. By minimizing thermal bridging, cold outdoor air is blocked from infiltrating attached garages, protecting vehicles, plumbing pipes, and rooms located directly above the garage.

Commercial Double Torsion Springs

Sub-zero cold stiffens steel and stresses garage door components. Maxon Doors outfits our residential doors with heavy-duty double torsion springs rather than cheap single-spring or extension spring configurations. This ensures balanced, smooth operation even on the coldest January mornings.

Sub-Zero Flexible Perimeter Seals

Our bottom rubber astragal and side jamb seals remain flexible at -40°C, conforming to concrete driveway variations and keeping out snowdrifts, driving rain, and prairie dust.
